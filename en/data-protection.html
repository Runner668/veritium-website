2. Hosting and server logs

When you visit this website, the hosting provider processes technically necessar y information such as your IP address, date and time of access, requested page, referrer URL, and browser and operating-system information. This processing is necessary to deliver the website securely and reliably and is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Log data is retained only for as long as necessary for these purposes or to meet legal obligations.

3. External resources and content delivery networks

This website loads JavaScript libraries from cdnjs. Your browser may therefore transmit connection data, including your IP address, to the respective providers. These resources support the presentation and functionality of the website and are used on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Contacting us

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide to handle your enquiry and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR, depending on the nature of your enquiry. We delete the data when the purpose ends unless statutory retention duties apply.

5. Consent management and Google Analytics

For the consent banner, we store your choice in local browser storage under the key veritium_cookie_consent_v1. This storage is necessary so that we can respect your decision on subsequent pages. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you have explicitly consented to the “Optional analytics” category. With consent, cookies such as _ga and _ga_<container-id> may be used to distinguish users and sessions. This may involve processing device and browser information, approximate location data and interactions with the website. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Without consent, the Analytics script is not loaded and Analytics cookies are not set. Advertising features and personalised advertising remain disabled. Google may process data outside the European Economic Area; the deployed configuration, retention period and transfer mechanism are reviewed regularly.

6. Your rights

Subject to the GDPR, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

7. Updates

We may update this notice when the website or applicable legal requirements change.
